How to make deposit?
Dial *565# on you mobile phone.

You can use your phone to dial *565*9# to make
deposit on your phone using USSD.

Use the deposit button the mobile app.

On the mobile App a deposit
button is provided to make deposit to your wallet.

Go to the bank to make deposit.

Go to any KPAY branch nearby to make you deposit

Go to the POS center to make deposit

Go to any POS center to make deposit.
You can go to any POS centre in your area to make deposit using the machine.
